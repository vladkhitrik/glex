Controls

  • Arrows or pad: steer, climb, descend.
  • Z and X: throttle up and down.
  • Hold C: reverse thrust.
  • T: gear up/down.
  • Y: cockpit or external view.
  • Q or Pause: pause the sim.
  • 1-4: change flaps.
  • Click an airport marker to spawn on the ground.
  • Wheel on the globe to zoom before flying.
